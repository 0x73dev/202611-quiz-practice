Privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026

The short version

  • This is a small, password-protected study site run by a small team.
  • When you open it, we record your IP address, approximate location, browser, device type and how you use the page, even before you enter the password.
  • Anything you type, including password attempts, is masked and never recorded. Your password never leaves your browser.
  • We use this to see whether the site is used and to spot unauthorised access. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.
  • You can ask us what we hold and have it deleted, by emailing legal.2026.alias@0x73.dev.

1Who we are

This site is operated by a small team in Ireland. The team decides what personal data is collected and why, which makes it the “controller” of that data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You can reach the team at legal.2026.alias@0x73.dev.

The site contains study material that is encrypted in your browser. It is shared with a small group of people. It is not a commercial service and has no accounts or sign-up.

We collect information automatically when your browser loads the page. We do not ask you for any personal details, and there is no form on the site other than the password box.

3How we use it

  • To see whether the site is being used, and roughly where and on what devices.
  • To notice unusual activity, such as repeated visits from an unexpected place, and to protect the password-protected content.
  • To understand which parts of the page people use, so the page can be improved.
  • To answer you if you contact us, and to handle a privacy request.

We do not use this information for advertising, we do not build marketing profiles, and we do not make decisions about you by automated means.

4Legal bases

Under the GDPR we need a legal basis to process personal data. We rely on:

  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) for analytics and security: the team has a genuine interest in knowing how the site is used and in protecting access to it. We have kept the data collected to what is needed for that, and masked typed text so that recordings capture as little as possible. You can object at any time (section 11).
  • Legal obligation or your request for handling emails and privacy requests that you send us.

6Cookies and browser storage

The site does not set analytics cookies and does not store analytics identifiers in your browser. Analytics identifiers are kept in the page’s memory and are discarded when you close or reload the tab, so a return visit looks like a new one. Your browser or your network may still keep ordinary technical data such as cache files.

7Session recordings

The analytics tool can record a visit as a replay, so the team can see how the page is used. To protect your privacy:

  • All text typed into input fields is masked, including password attempts. It is replaced with placeholder characters before it leaves your browser.
  • The password you enter is used inside your browser to unlock the page and is never sent to us or to any provider.
  • What is shown on the page after it is unlocked, such as the study questions, can appear in a recording.
  • Recordings are viewed only by the team and are not shared.
If you do not want to be recorded, you can block the analytics scripts (section 12) or simply not visit the page.

8International transfers

Visit records and recordings are stored on PostHog’s EU cloud. Cloudflare and GitHub operate globally, so some request data may be processed outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. These providers rely on recognised safeguards for such transfers, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. See their own privacy notices for the details.

9How long we keep it

Visit records and session recordings are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to deal with your request. We will delete your data sooner if you ask us to and we can identify it (section 11).

10Security

  • The study content is encrypted with AES-256 and decrypted only in your browser once the correct password is entered.
  • The site is served over HTTPS.
  • Access to the analytics dashboards is limited to the team and protected by the providers’ sign-in.

No online service is perfectly secure. If a breach affecting your data occurred, we would act as the law requires.

11Your rights

If you are in the EU, the UK or a similar jurisdiction, you can ask us to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct data that is wrong.
  • Delete your data.
  • Restrict or object to our use of your data, in particular where we rely on legitimate interests.
  • Receive data you provided in a portable form, where that applies.

To make a request, email legal.2026.alias@0x73.dev. Because we do not know who you are, please include your IP address and the approximate date and time of your visit so we can find the records. We may ask for a little more information to confirm the records are yours. We aim to reply within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the supervisory authority. In Ireland this is the Data Protection Commission.

12Blocking and opting out

  • Most ad blockers and tracker-blocking browsers stop the analytics scripts from loading. The site works the same way without them.
  • Browsers with a “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control” setting may also reduce tracking, but we cannot promise that every setting is honoured.
  • You can email us to ask that your data be removed.

13Children

The site is intended for students preparing for a school science quiz, so some visitors may be under 18. We do not knowingly ask for personal information from anyone, and we do not profile or target young people. If you are a parent or guardian and want data about a child deleted, email us.

14Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will make that clear on the page’s lock screen.

15Contact

Questions, requests or complaints about this policy: legal.2026.alias@0x73.dev.
